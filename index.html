Your ads × your flow

Each of your ads sells one story. The app should open on it.

In the Meta Ad Library, the LINE WEBTOON page runs about 170 active ads in the US. The 30 we read each sell a single series and link to the App Store, Google Play or a WEBTOON app link.

We can’t see what a new install meets after these ads. Your App Store listing shows a genre screen, “What kind of stories are you into?”, and the ad has already answered it. So the prototype opens on the ad, picks the genres for them, and puts that series first.

Apple Ads, for the same logic: account-level keyword research for the US (5 Oct – 5 Nov 2026) shows WEBTOON on “solo leveling” (2% share of voice), “tapas” (2%) and another app’s name, “tapas – comics and novels” (3%). Apple doesn’t say which of your apps each ad promoted. A search for one series is the same intent as these Meta ads, and deserves the same first screen.
